For:

- Hero images
- About images

# STYLE B — CIRCULAR PORTRAITS

For:

- Lawyers
- Advocates
- Team members

Use perfectly circular portraits where appropriate.

The image container should:

- Maintain a 1:1 aspect ratio
- Use object-fit: cover
- Keep the face centered
- Prevent stretching

Example:

border-radius: 9999px;
overflow: hidden;

Do not force every image on the website into a circle.

Use circles specifically for people where they improve the visual hierarchy.
